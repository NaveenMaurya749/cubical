\section{Introduction}

\textit{Type Theory}, and in particular, its variants such as Homotopy Type Theory, Cubical Type Theory etc.
are foundational systems for mathematics, alternative to ZF or other set theories.
However, as opposed to any of the usual set-theoretic foundations, which consists of a number of axioms,
defining sets and how they behave, in the language of first-order logic, which is separately assumed to be 
established beforehand.
